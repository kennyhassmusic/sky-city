# SkyCity game pack: credits

This pack is used only in the SkyCity game (sky-city `gen/game-pack`). None of it is part of the building-types
generator, its starter pack or anything sold with it. The meshes were converted (scaled, re-pivoted, decimated,
textures packed and resized) by `Scripts/userpack/convert_blender.py`; the changes are ours, the originals are the
authors' below.

## NVIDIA Physical AI SimReady Warehouse 01

"NVIDIA Physical AI SimReady Warehouse 01" (v1.1.0) by NVIDIA Corporation, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-SimReady-Warehouse-01

Used: SM_Forklift_C01_Blue_01, SM_Forklift_C01_Red_01, SM_BlockPallet_A01_WoodNewA_01 (stacked), Cardbox_B2,
SM_PlywoodCrate_B01_01, SM_HeavyDutyPalletTruck_A01_01, SM_IBCTank_A02_01, SM_SteelDrum_A01_01, SM_SteelDrum_A02_01.
Modified: converted from USD to GLB, decimated, UDIM textures packed into an atlas, pallets and cartons assembled
into stacks and loads.

## Scenario 3D Collection (AI-generated)

"Scenario 3D Collection" by Scenario, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
All of these assets are AI-generated.
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/scenario-labs/3d-collection

Used: scenario:asset_AXM18qzFnbRcKpKdoZsCZ85y and scenario:asset_yDcD8diFQzffCwnHSsJVoPRV (off-road SUVs),
ce679cb83f448b83 (1950s pickup), scenario:asset_U2KQ686opM4MA4YiuxrvK1Zh (person in a hoodie),
scenario:asset_u35vcY44mBmSGNaxAnjf67VD (e-bike).
Modified: decimated, rescaled to real dimensions (per axis), turned to face +X, textures resized.
No example made with a Tencent Hunyuan model is used (none is in this collection; see curation.json).
