Landmarks, parks and artworks
  All landmark, park, bridge and building models are original work, made from OpenStreetMap data, public-domain
  aerials, published measurements and reference photos. No third-party geometry or textures were copied. The Texas
  Capitol follows the Historic American Buildings Survey drawings (Library of Congress, public domain).
  Artworks still in copyright appear only as original, simplified stand-ins, not reproductions. These include Cloud Gate
  (Anish Kapoor), the Jay Pritzker Pavilion and BP Bridge (Frank Gehry), Crown Fountain (Jaume Plensa; generic faces),
  Flamingo (Alexander Calder), The Four Seasons (Marc Chagall), Miro's Chicago (Joan Miro) and the Austin statues.
  Public-domain sculptures (the Art Institute lions, The Bowman and The Spearman, the Saint-Gaudens Lincoln and Logan
  monuments, the Chicago Picasso and others) are modelled in simplified form.
  Names of real places, buildings, bridges and parks identify the setting only. They belong to their owners, who don't
  sponsor or endorse this game.
