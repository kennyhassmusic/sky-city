=====================================================================
AUSTIN - Interstate 35
=====================================================================

austin_i35_upperdeck_below_night_01.jpg
  Shows: I-35 upper deck seen from the lower-level frontage road just north of downtown (near the
         MLK / 26th St "LBJ Library" exit), long exposure. Right: single rectangular column with a wide
         hammerhead cap carrying about 7 prestressed I-girders. Left: the other direction's upper deck
         with a galvanized box-truss sign bridge on it. Lower main lanes in a cut with a vertical
         retaining wall; cross-street bridge behind.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Street_-_Flickr_-_Night_Photo.jpg
  Author: Maxx@night (Flickr "Night Photo"), taken 4 Dec 2021.  Licence: CC BY 2.0

austin_i35_upperdeck_below_night_02.jpg
  Shows: same double-deck area (green "EXIT 236B" sign). Right: upper-deck single-column T-bent,
         cap and the soffit with about 7 I-girders. Left: opposite upper deck on single columns.
         Lower deck with W-beam guardrail on a concrete curb in front.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flow_-_Flickr_-_Night_Photo.jpg
  Author: Maxx@night, taken 9 Dec 2022.  Licence: CC BY 2.0

austin_i35_downtown_mainlanes_top_04.jpg
  Shows: downtown I-35 main lanes from a cross-street bridge (around 11th/12th St), looking south
         toward the elevated section. Four lanes each way plus an exit lane, concrete safety-shape
         median barrier, grass side slopes, overhead green guide sign "EXIT 234B Cesar Chavez St /
         Holly St", tall light poles along the top of the slope.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Typical_Weekend_Traffic_on_IH_35_Downtown_Austin_2019.jpg
  Author: Larry D. Moore, 17 Feb 2019.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

austin_i35_downtown_dusk_top_05.jpg
  Shows: I-35 main lanes at the downtown edge at dusk, with a 2-lane elevated roadway on the left.
         Shows the concrete median barrier, twin-arm (double davit) light poles and the downtown
         skyline. Photo taken in 2010.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AustinInterstate35Skyline-May2010.JPG
  Author: LoneStarMike, 2010.  Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0

austin_i35_ladybirdlake_night_lights_06.jpg
  Shows: I-35 at Lady Bird Lake at night, looking north to downtown. Concrete safety-shape median
         barrier, twin-arm davit light poles about 12-14 m tall (photo estimate), and the frontage
         road at a lower level on the right.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Austin_(8095567898).jpg
  Author: Aleksandr Zykov, 10 Oct 2012.  Licence: CC BY-SA 2.0

austin_other_modern_elevated_NOT_i35_07.jpg   (CONTRAST ONLY - NOT I-35)
  Shows: a modern Austin elevated expressway (location not stated; tags "highway, elevated, atx";
         probably the 2020s US 183 South / Bergstrom Expressway - this is an estimate). Twin decks on
         trapezoidal TxDOT U-beams, paired rusticated columns with flared capitals. The 1970s I-35
         decks look nothing like this: use it only as a "future" contrast.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Support_-_Flickr_-_Night_Photo.jpg
  Author: Maxx@night, 22 Jan 2023.  Licence: CC BY 2.0

=====================================================================
CHICAGO
=====================================================================

chicago_danryan_viaduct_below_ruble18th_01.jpg
  Shows: the Dan Ryan (I-90/94) elevated viaduct seen from Ruble St at 18th St. Multi-column
         concrete bents (round columns, 6+ per bent) under a deep cap beam, a pale concrete parapet
         over a darker girder band, black drain downspouts on the columns, and a galvanized sign
         bridge with green guide signs (I-90/94 West, I-290 exits 51H-I). City cobra-head poles at
         street level.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ruble_St_north_at_18th_St_-_Chicago,_IL_-_June_2022.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 27 Jun 2022.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_danryan_viaduct_girder_gantry_canalport_02.jpg
  Shows: close side view of the Dan Ryan westbound viaduct at Canalport Ave. Rust/orange-brown steel
         plate-girder fascia (weathered paint or weathering steel, not identified) under a pale,
         stained concrete parapet with a drip groove. A brown-painted steel sign bridge on base
         plates on the parapet, with green signs and I-90/94 and I-290 shields. Black street-light
         pole; a high-mast tower in the distance.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:I-90_%26_I-94_WB_at_Canalport_Ave_-_September_2021.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 3 Sep 2021.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_danryan_elevated_top_gantries_canalport_03.jpg
  Shows: driver's view on top of the Dan Ryan viaduct (westbound at Canalport Ave). Concrete
         parapets, two brown steel frame sign structures (one cantilevered), a high-mast light tower
         (about 30-40 m, estimate) and the skyline.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:I-90_I-94_west_at_Canalport_Ave_ramp_-_Chicago,_IL_-_December_2021.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 18 Dec 2021.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_chinatown_feeder_ramps_below_lasalle_04.jpg
  Shows: Chinatown feeder ramp structures crossing overhead at LaSalle St. Weathered concrete
         parapet with graffiti, slate-gray steel girders, square concrete piers with caps and round
         columns, and single-arm davit light poles on the parapet. A second viaduct behind has
         maroon girders and steel bents. Concrete median barrier and retaining walls below.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chinatown_feeder_ramp_at_LaSalle_St_-_Chicago,_IL_-_November_2025.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 8 Nov 2025.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_ohio_feeder_steel_girders_kingsbury_05.jpg
  Shows: the Ohio Street feeder (Kennedy spur) over Kingsbury St. Shallow multi-girder steel deck
         with maroon/red-brown girders, concrete parapet with a metal pipe railing on top, concrete
         pier walls and steel multi-column bents, amber lights under the deck, a Chicago curved-arm
         street light on the pier and a green guide sign on the deck.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kingsbury_St_at_Ohio_St_feeder_ramp_-_Chicago,_IL_-_May_2023.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 20 May 2023.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_ohio_feeder_side_frontage_06.jpg
  Shows: the Ohio feeder viaduct along its frontage street. Red-brown girder band under a
         beige-gray concrete parapet and fascia; the space under the deck is enclosed or used for
         parking; a davit light pole on the deck; River North towers.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ohio_St_feeder_ramp_and_frontage_road_-_Chicago,_IL_-_May_2023.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 20 May 2023.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_circle_interchange_flyovers_aerial_07.jpg
  Shows: aerial of the Circle (Jane Byrne) Interchange from Willis Tower in 2018, during
         reconstruction. Curved flyover ramps on single-column piers, stacked levels, the Blue Line
         in the Eisenhower median, and a new flyover under construction.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chicago_Circle_Interchange_2018.jpg
  Author: Chris6d, 18 Jul 2018.  Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0

chicago_stevenson_i55_elevated_kingdr_truss_08.jpg
  Shows: I-55 Stevenson northbound near its east end at King Dr. A galvanized (bare silver)
         four-chord box-truss sign bridge with green guide signs and US 41 shields. The elevated
         McCormick Place structure on the left; davit poles beyond.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:I-55_NB_at_King_Dr_(exit_293D)_-_Chicago,_IL_-_December_2021.jpg
  Author: AlphaBeta135, 4 Dec 2021.  Licence: CC BY 4.0

chicago_stevenson_i55_canal_aerial_09.jpg
  Shows: aerial of the Stevenson (I-55) running beside the Sanitary & Ship Canal and rail yards on
         the Southwest Side. Mostly at grade here: useful for the corridor layout, not for viaduct
         detail.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Industrial_Canal_and_I-55_in_Chicago.jpg
  Author: formulanone, 13 Jul 2020.  Licence: CC BY-SA 2.0
