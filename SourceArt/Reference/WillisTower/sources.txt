Facts (web pages viewed, not saved):
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willis_Tower  - 108 storeys, roof 442 m (1,451 ft), top floor 413 m, Skydeck 103rd floor 412.4 m;
      nine 75 ft (22.86 m) tubes, 225 ft (68.6 m) square; NW+SE end at floor 50, NE+SW at 66, N+E+S at 90, W+C to 108;
      antennas 1982, west antenna extended 2000 to 527 m (1,729 ft); black anodised aluminium and bronze-tinted glass,
      16,000 windows 5 x 8 ft, columns 15 ft (4.57 m) on centre; black louvre bands at floors 29-32, 64-65, 88-89, 104-108;
      The Ledge: glass boxes ~4 ft (1.2-1.3 m) out from the 103rd-floor west facade over Wacker Drive (4 in 2009, a 5th in 2022);
      Catalog (Gensler, 2017-22): 3-storey annex on the south and west replacing the plaza, roof garden ~30,000 sq ft.
    https://skyscraperpage.com/b5  - floor levels (Chicago Building Dept.): 50th 658 ft, 66th 874 ft, 90th 1,186 ft,
      103rd deck 1,353 ft; main roof deck 1,432 ft (blueprints), roof 1,451 ft; 225 ft between column lines, 229 ft at the outside faces;
      west antenna 1,730 ft, east antenna 1,710 ft; Franklin St entrance 4 ft below Wacker.
