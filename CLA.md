Version 1.0, 21 October 2026

Thank you for contributing to Sky City. This agreement lets Kenny Hass Music LLC ("**the Project Owner**") use your contribution in the game, including in versions that are sold, while you keep the copyright in your work. It is modelled on widely used open-source contributor agreements. Please read it before you contribute: by submitting a contribution, and by signing as described at the end, you accept it.

- **"You"** means the person (or the legal entity, if you sign on behalf of one) submitting a Contribution.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship that you intentionally submit to the Sky City project, including code, scripts, art, 3D models, textures, sounds, data, text and documentation. Submission includes pull requests, issues, comments with attachments, and anything else sent to the project's repositories or maintainers for inclusion.

## 4. What you confirm

You confirm that:

1. Each Contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement.
2. If your employer or anyone else has rights to work you create, they have allowed you to make the Contribution under this agreement, or have waived those rights.

The Project Owner does not have to use your Contribution. Unless you say otherwise in writing, you provide your Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind. You don't have to provide support for them.

## 6. Credit

The project will keep credits for Contributions in the repository's history and, where practical, in the in-game or bundled credits.

## How to sign

The first time you open a pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply in the pull request with:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your signature is recorded with your GitHub username and covers all your future Contributions to Sky City. If you contribute on behalf of a company, contact the Project Owner first.
