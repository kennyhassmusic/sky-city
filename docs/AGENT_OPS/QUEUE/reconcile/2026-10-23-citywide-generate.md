- What: generator over every mashup lot and driveable road, then LOD0 meshes for a Chicago-core cluster (22 buildings, the script cap) and one Austin street where a high tier sits beside mid. Every facade face is projected from its own width and height, U and V at one scale. Windows match the 24×19 lit rect. Leftover length is a pilaster. The build fails if texel density or window aspect is outside 3%. Glazed floors stay a curtain wall. Import script brings the GLBs into `/Game/Maps/SkyCityGenerated`. The mesher prints `bpy.app.version`, accepts only Blender 5.2.x LTS on Python 3.13, and exits 1 on a Python warning, a deprecated-API message, or a missing node or socket. One unit is one metre and glTF is 1:1. Booleans use EXACT and are read through the depsgraph. The census fails the build when a building's base is not on the sidewalk or its footprint or height misses the plan. Six-view Workbench sheets cover the tall tower, the street anchor and one house. Facade stains are procedural: curvature on edges, ambient occlusion in corners, world-aligned drips under sills and ledges, dirt at the base, scaled by district age and tier. The leak card is not used on walls. The Unreal side is `M_Facade` plus one instance per wall slot.
