## Contribute

Sky City's code and art are open for noncommercial use: read it, fix it, add landmarks, cars and races. It's all on GitHub: https://github.com/kennyhassmusic/sky-city

Building with an AI assistant? **Hand `AGENTS.md` to your AI.** It sits in the top folder of the repo and tells your AI how to set up, where everything lives, the rules, and a few easy first jobs. You clone the repo (run `git lfs install` first) and add Epic's free City Sample. You don't need the Art Pack to contribute.

## Art Pack

Want to use Sky City's art in your own project? Download **Sky City Art Pack (free for noncommercial use)** from the downloads on this page: Unreal Engine 5.8 assets plus the original GLB, PNG and WAV files. It's free for noncommercial use (CC BY-NC 4.0), **with credit**:

> Art by Kenny Hass Music LLC, from Sky City (https://kornontheken.itch.io/sky-city)

Selling it, or using it in something you sell, needs written permission: message Kenny Hass Music LLC through this page.

Monetized streams and videos of Sky City are OK too.

The pack doesn't include the Skyster Roadster, Epic's City Sample, licensed car models, or the Sky City and Skyster names and logos.
