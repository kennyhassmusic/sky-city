# Sky City

**An open-world driving and flying sandbox.** Chicago's Loop and lakefront are stitched to downtown Austin, with a Texas rocket test site out on the edge of town. Drive, boost, wall-ride and finally fly the Skyster Roadster through all of it.

## What's in it
- **Two cities in one:** about 3.6 × 3.6 km of streets, built from real map data. There's Lake Shore Drive, Congress Avenue, the river and the lake, with traffic, signals and flying robotaxis overhead.
- **Landmarks to find:** Buckingham Fountain, Navy Pier and its wheel, the Bean, the Texas Capitol, the elevated L (you can drive on its tracks) and more.
- **The rocket site:** a recreation of the McGregor, Texas test site and the night the Roadster was revealed. There's the ramp jump, the kickflip over the line-up, and engine tests that light up the sky.
- **A car that grows with you:** 6 levels unlock boost, double jumps, wall-riding, hovering and full flight. Fuel add-ons stretch your flight time.
- **Self-driving mode, a mini map, chargers** and other drivable vehicles.

## Made for fun, built to grow
Sky City is a passion project, made to be fun to play and easy to add to. It's an early build: expect rough edges, blocky stand-ins and things that change. The source is going up on GitHub soon, so anyone can add places, cars, races and secrets. It's free for everyone to play, mod, share, stream and contribute to. The one thing reserved is selling the game itself.

**Want to build on it? The simple way:**
1. Get **Unreal Engine 5.8** (free, from the Epic Games Launcher) on an Apple Silicon Mac.
2. Download the **Sky City source** from GitHub (link coming soon) and Epic's free **City Sample** from Fab.
3. Open `SkyCity.uproject`. Most of the world is generated by scripts from OpenStreetMap: change a script, rerun it, and the city rebuilds.
4. We built it with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code) driving the Unreal editor, and that's still the easiest way in. Describe what you want, check it in the editor, and refine.
5. Share it back with a pull request. Use your own work or freely licensed assets (CC0 / CC BY) and no real brand names or logos.

## Requirements
- An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer). Intel Macs and Windows aren't supported yet.
- About 6 GB of free disk space.
- An Xbox controller (recommended) or keyboard. The controls are shown in the game's help screen.
