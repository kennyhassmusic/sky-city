# Sky City: places to check out, things to do, easter eggs

Started 20 Oct 2026 from Kenny's ideas, to grow into the game's goals and achievements. Names on screen stay
generic: no carmaker or rocket-company names.

## Kenny's first two
- **Drive up the Willis Tower.** Wall-ride all 442 m up the face to the roof, and park in one of the Skydeck's glass
  "Ledge" boxes on the 103rd floor. The tower is being given its real look tonight.
- **Baked Beans.** Touch Cloud Gate (the Bean) with any thruster's plume. The mirror glows hot orange and slowly cools,
  and the achievement pops.

## More places and challenges (ideas to pick from)
Chicago
- **Ride the L:** drive a full lap of the Loop on the elevated tracks. Time trial; bonus for passing through all 7
  stations.
- **Pier Pressure:** launch off the far end of Navy Pier and land on the harbor lighthouse breakwater.
- **Wheel of Fortune:** fly through the Centennial Wheel's rim between two gondolas.
- **Car Wash:** drive through Buckingham Fountain's 46 m centre jet during the night show.
- **Lock and Load:** drive through the Chicago Harbor Lock end to end.
- **Lake Shore top speed:** hit 230 mph on Lake Shore Drive.
- **Lion's Share:** jump between the two lions in front of the Art Institute.
- **Pritzker hang time:** wall-ride the Pritzker Pavilion's steel trellis.

Austin
- **Goddess Touch:** land on the Capitol dome beside the Goddess of Liberty (92 m).
- **Great Walk:** drive up Congress Avenue, the Great Walk and the south steps in one go.
- **Bat Bridge:** be on the Congress Avenue Bridge at dusk when the bats come out. A bat swarm is to build.
- **Walk on Water:** skim across Lady Bird Lake at speed.
- **Jaywalker:** cross the Pfluger pedestrian bridge.
- **Moonlight Tour:** touch every moonlight tower.
- **Upper Deck Drop:** jump from I-35's upper deck to the lanes below.

McGregor
- **The Reveal:** the ramp jump and the kickflip over the line-up, as at the real event.
- **Too Hot:** drive through a live booster test's plume. Hull damage and a scorched paint look.
- **Grasshopper:** a vertical hover take-off from the old Grasshopper pad.
- **Tower Climb:** wall-ride the 84 m water tower.

Everywhere
- Charge at every fast charger.
- Find every boost add-on: nitrogen, combustion, rocket.
- Land on every landmark roof.
