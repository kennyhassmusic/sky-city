# Code of Conduct

Sky City is a project for people who love driving, flying and building cool things. We want everyone, from first-time modders to seasoned engineers, to feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone was new once.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This shader is too expensive on M1" is useful. "This is garbage" isn't.
- **Assume good intent,** and ask before assuming otherwise.
- **Give credit.** Say who made what, including in asset credits.
- **Respect decisions.** Disagree openly, then commit once a decision is made.

## What's not OK

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind, including on race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or nationality
- Sexual content or unwanted advances
- Sharing someone's private information without consent
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Knowingly submitting stolen, ripped or improperly licensed content

## Where this applies

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), on the community chat, at playtests and events, and anywhere you represent Sky City.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks this code, contact the maintainers privately: use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's Security tab (it reaches only the maintainers, and works for conduct reports too), or message Kenny through his itch.io page. Reports are kept confidential.

## Consequences

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, in proportion to what happened. That may be a private warning, a temporary ban, or permanent removal from the project and its spaces. Maintainers who don't follow or enforce this code may lose their role.
